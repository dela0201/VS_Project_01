Programmes at Belgium Campus

Higher Certificate in IT

NQF 5 · 1 year · Foundation route

  • Best for: building basics + bridging into the Diploma
  • You’ll cover: intro programming, networks, databases, problem-solving
  • Great if you want a structured starting point

Diploma in IT

NQF 6 · 3 years · Practical + job-ready

  • Best for: hands-on skills and real projects
  • You’ll cover: software development, systems, databases, teamwork
  • Often includes workplace-style learning (WIL)

BSc in IT

NQF 7 · 3 years · Deeper theory + growth

  • Best for: strong fundamentals and long-term career options
  • You’ll cover: advanced programming, computing concepts, analytics
  • Good choice if you may want postgrad later
